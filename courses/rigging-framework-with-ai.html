Building Your Own Rigging Framework with AI

MayaPythonClaude12 modules

Build your own Maya rigging framework from an empty folder, with Claude as your coding partner. No programming background needed.

What you will build

By the end of the course you own a working rigging framework, and you can rebuild your own character or prop from a clean scene with one command.

A framework is more than an auto-rig. Yours will cover the whole workflow: naming rules, Python classes that wrap Maya nodes, reusable rig components such as FK chains and IK/FK arms, data that survives every rebuild, and simple tools that artists use every day.

Who it is for

Riggers and technical artists who know Maya but have never built a framework of their own.

  • Riggers who rig by hand or with someone else's auto-rig and want their own system.
  • Technical artists with a few MEL or Python snippets who want to grow them into real tools.
  • Rigging leads who want a pipeline their team can maintain and extend with AI.

You don't need to know how to code. Claude writes the first draft of the code, and you learn to read it, test it in Maya and direct the next change.

What you will learn

The course has 12 modules in four parts. Each part builds on the one before it.

PartWhat you build
1. FoundationYour workspace, a naming convention, and Python classes that work with Maya nodes and attributes
2. Rig componentsA rig base, then components from a simple FK chain to a full arm and leg
3. Data and build pipelineSaving guides, control shapes and skin weights, and a build script that rebuilds the rig
4. Tool UIsSimple Qt tools that let artists build rigs and move data without writing code

How it works

Every session follows the same loop: understand the rigging idea, ask Claude to build it, test it in Maya, then make it your own. You learn to work with AI the way a lead works with a junior: give clear direction, review the result, and stay in charge of the design.

Each module ends with a lab, and every lab adds a working piece to your framework. For the final project, you rebuild your own asset from a clean scene with one command.

What you need

  • Autodesk Maya 2022 or later, and comfort rigging by hand.
  • Visual Studio Code and a Claude Pro plan or higher. We set both up together in the first session.
  • One character or prop you want to rig for your final project.

Format

12 weekly sessions of 3 hours, plus a final demo session. Classes are capped at 12 students, so every framework gets reviewed by the instructor.
