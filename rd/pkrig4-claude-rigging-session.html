Prompt-Driven Rigging — a pkrig4 session with Claude

Mayapkrig4ClaudengSkinToolsBlender

A pkrig4 rigging session in Maya, driven entirely by prompts to Claude. Starting from a pkrig3 skinning ROM, Claude converts it to the new pkrig4 rig, rebuilds lost ngSkinTools layers, fixes influences that weren't deforming, adjusts the hand guide, slims down the arm bendy joints, and ports the finished rig to Blender.

Every step is a plain-English prompt: no manual scripting, just reviewing the results in the viewport and asking for the next change. Claude's working time is sped up.
